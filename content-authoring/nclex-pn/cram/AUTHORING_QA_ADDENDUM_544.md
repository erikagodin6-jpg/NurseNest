## New neurologic/procedure safety batch

Ten lessons add serial neurologic checks, post-stroke dysphagia safety, EVD monitoring, seizure precautions, lumbar-puncture aftercare, post-craniotomy deterioration, spinal-cord precautions, autonomic dysreflexia, neurogenic bladder/bowel safety, and acute intracerebral-hemorrhage monitoring.

High-risk rules preserved:
- neurologic trend changes require prompt escalation rather than waiting for the next routine interval;
- acute stroke oral intake follows the dysphagia-screening pathway and silent aspiration remains possible;
- EVD leveling/clamping/handling follows neurocritical-care protocol and is not universal PN authority;
- seizure care prohibits forceful restraint or objects in the mouth;
- post-craniotomy new focal deficit or decreasing consciousness is never normalized as routine sedation;
- autonomic dysreflexia is treated as a hypertensive emergency pattern;
- current AHA/ASA ICH guidance does not support routine prophylactic antiseizure medication without seizure evidence.

## New newborn/neonatal safety batch

Ten lessons add newborn transition, thermoregulation, hypoglycemia, hyperbilirubinemia, feeding adequacy, safe sleep, umbilical-cord care, circumcision aftercare, newborn screening, and preterm discharge/car-seat safety.

Current-source rules preserved:
- 2025 AHA/AAP neonatal resuscitation guidance makes effective ventilation the priority when a newborn requires resuscitation;
- bilirubin interpretation uses gestational age, postnatal age in hours, bilirubin value, and neurotoxicity risk rather than visual jaundice alone;
- safe sleep remains supine on a firm flat non-inclined surface with soft items excluded;
- newborn feeding adequacy is assessed by transfer, output, weight/hydration, and clinical status rather than minutes feeding;
- screening does not replace diagnostic evaluation of a symptomatic newborn;
- a car-seat observation evaluates travel positioning, not permission for routine sleep in sitting devices.
