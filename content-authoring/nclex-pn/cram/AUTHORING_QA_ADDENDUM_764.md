## New palliative/end-of-life batch

Ten lessons add comfort-focused pain and dyspnea care, terminal secretions, nausea/constipation, agitation/delirium, mouth care, skin comfort, medication-review awareness, goals-of-care handoff, family/caregiver support, and actively dying changes.

Safety rules preserved:
- Hospice enrollment does not eliminate assessment, medication safety, or escalation of uncontrolled symptoms.
- Comfort goals guide monitoring intensity, but sudden new distress is still assessed for reversible danger when consistent with goals.
- Opioids, anxiolytics, anticholinergics, and other comfort medications remain order/protocol governed and require response monitoring.
- PN/LVN does not independently discontinue medications, create medical orders for goals/code status, pronounce death where not authorized, or replace hospice/prescriber decision-making.
- Family teaching distinguishes expected dying changes from uncontrolled pain, dyspnea, bleeding, agitation, or caregiver safety problems.

## New cardiac-device/rhythm-procedure batch

Ten lessons add pacemaker aftercare, ICD-shock safety, device-pocket infection, lead displacement, temporary-pacing malfunction, CIED procedural handoff, elective-cardioversion preparation, post-cardioversion monitoring, post-ablation deterioration, and telemetry lead/artifact verification.

Safety rules preserved:
- Monitor alarms and pacing spikes are correlated with the patient and pulse; artifact is not treated as a diagnosis.
- Repeated ICD shocks, syncope, unstable rhythm, severe bradycardia, or loss of mechanical capture trigger urgent escalation.
- Device programming, magnet strategy, anticoagulation decisions, and electrophysiology procedural management remain with authorized device/cardiology teams.
- Elective cardioversion preparation explicitly flags anticoagulation-plan discrepancies instead of independently resolving them.
- Post-ablation hypotension prompts assessment for bleeding and pericardial complications rather than being dismissed as sedation recovery.

## Source refresh

The palliative tranche was refreshed against CMS hospice guidance current through July 2026. Cardiac-device/rhythm content was refreshed against current American Heart Association cardioversion, ICD/CIED, and periprocedural device-management guidance.
