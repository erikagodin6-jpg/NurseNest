## New transfusion/blood-product batch

Ten lessons cover positive patient/component identification, early monitoring, suspected-reaction response, acute hemolytic reaction, TACO, TRALI, allergic reaction, septic reaction, febrile nonhemolytic reaction, and transfusion-history handoff.

Safety rules preserved:
- Any bedside identity/compatibility mismatch stops the process before transfusion.
- New symptoms during transfusion are treated as a possible transfusion reaction until evaluated.
- Suspected reaction response stops the implicated component and activates the facility reaction protocol rather than merely slowing the infusion.
- TACO and TRALI remain distinct clinical syndromes; respiratory symptoms are not assigned a diagnosis from one cue alone.
- Fever/rigors trigger evaluation for hemolysis and bacterial contamination before benign classification.
- Prior reaction/antibody/special-component history follows the patient through handoffs.
- PN/LVN transfusion participation remains state/facility/competency dependent.

## New ostomy/drainage/wound-device batch

Ten lessons cover stoma perfusion, high-output ileostomy dehydration, peristomal skin injury, ostomy obstruction, prolapse/retraction, closed-suction drains, nephrostomy drainage, biliary drains, drain dislodgement, and output-trend escalation.

Safety rules preserved:
- Dark/dusky stoma tissue is a perfusion concern, not routine bruising.
- High-output ileostomy is evaluated for dehydration/renal loss rather than treated as only a pouching problem.
- Sudden cessation of ostomy/drain output is not assumed to mean improvement.
- Dislodged drains are not blindly reinserted.
- Nephrostomy/biliary-drain flushing, capping, and medication instillation remain order/procedure specific.
- State/facility-variable procedure authority remains tagged.
