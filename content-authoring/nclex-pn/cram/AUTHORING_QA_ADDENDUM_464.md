## Respiratory / allergy pharmacology QA

The 46-series batch preserves maintenance-versus-rescue distinctions. Tiotropium HandiHaler capsules are inhaled through the device and are not swallowed; tiotropium, umeclidinium/vilanterol, nebulized formoterol, oral roflumilast, and severe-asthma biologics are not presented as rapid rescue therapy. Budesonide-containing products retain oral-candidiasis/systemic-steroid monitoring and mouth-rinse teaching. Roflumilast retains psychiatric-event/suicidality and clinically significant weight-loss monitoring. Omalizumab retains boxed anaphylaxis risk. Mepolizumab, benralizumab, and tezepelumab remain phenotype/add-on maintenance therapies, not substitutes for acute stabilization. Biologic administration authority remains state/facility/competency dependent.

Current source refreshes include DailyMed/FDA labeling available through 2026-08, including roflumilast updated 2026-07, Symbicort 2026-03, budesonide inhalation suspension 2026-02, formoterol solution 2026-03, Fasenra 2026-05, and current Xolair/Nucala/Tezspire labeling.

## GI / hepatology pharmacology QA

The 47-series batch keeps hepatic encephalopathy therapy clinically anchored: lactulose response is cognition/stool balance rather than indiscriminate diarrhea production, and worsening encephalopathy still requires evaluation for precipitating causes. Rifaximin is recurrence-prevention therapy rather than a substitute for acute assessment. Ursodiol is chronic bile-acid therapy and not source control for acute cholangitis/obstruction. Cholestyramine retains clinically important binding/separation interactions with other oral medications.

Linaclotide, lubiprostone, dicyclomine, and prucalopride require obstruction screening rather than reflexively treating severe abdominal symptoms as uncomplicated constipation or spasm. Dicyclomine retains anticholinergic delirium/retention/glaucoma risk. Oral budesonide is not treated as free of systemic corticosteroid effects. Vedolizumab retains serious-infection, hypersensitivity/infusion, hepatic, and new-neurologic-symptom vigilance. Prucalopride retains mood/suicidality monitoring.

Current source refreshes include 2026 lactulose labeling, ursodiol updated 2026-07, Entyvio updated 2026-04, and current FDA-approved labeling for rifaximin, cholestyramine, linaclotide, lubiprostone, dicyclomine, oral budesonide, and prucalopride.
