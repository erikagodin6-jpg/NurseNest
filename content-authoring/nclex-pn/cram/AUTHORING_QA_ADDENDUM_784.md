## Oncology emergency/symptom safety

Ten lessons add neutropenic fever, tumor lysis syndrome, malignant spinal cord compression, superior vena cava syndrome, cancer-related hypercalcemia, mucositis, thrombocytopenia bleeding precautions, vesicant extravasation, cancer-pain reassessment, and malignant pericardial-effusion/tamponade recognition.

Safety constraints preserved:
- urgent oncologic deterioration is escalated rather than managed as routine fatigue, pain, constipation, or edema;
- no autonomous chemotherapy, vesicant, anticoagulation, electrolyte, or oncologic prescribing authority is implied;
- suspected vesicant extravasation fails closed to stop-and-protocol workflow;
- neurologic weakness, sepsis physiology, arrhythmia, and obstructive-shock cues are treated as emergency findings.

## Preventive/immunization safety

Ten lessons add vaccine screening, vaccination anaphylaxis readiness, cold-chain handling, immunization-record reconciliation, screening-versus-diagnostic routing, tobacco cessation, preventive fall review, sexual-health screening, skin-cancer prevention, and preventive medication review.

Safety constraints preserved:
- symptomatic patients are never routed as routine screening cases;
- vaccine contraindications and schedules remain product/current-guidance specific;
- anaphylaxis treatment is not replaced by antihistamine-only care;
- medication review identifies and escalates problems without PN/LVN autonomous deprescribing or prescribing.
