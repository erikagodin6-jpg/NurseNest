## New chest-tube / pleural-drainage batch

Ten lessons add drainage-system position, air-leak troubleshooting, abrupt drainage changes, tube dislodgement, tension-pneumothorax recognition, transport safety, subcutaneous emphysema, post-removal monitoring, empyema/drainage infection cues, and pain/deep-breathing safety.

Required safeguards:
- drainage unit remains upright and below chest level;
- routine/prolonged clamping is not taught;
- sudden dyspnea or tension physiology triggers emergency escalation;
- dislodged tubes are never blindly reinserted;
- abrupt drainage increases or decreases are interpreted in clinical context;
- routine stripping is not taught;
- pain treatment is balanced against respiratory-sedation monitoring.

## New central-vascular-access batch

Ten lessons add dressing integrity, hub disinfection, CLABSI recognition, occlusion, migration/dislodgement, catheter-associated thrombosis, air embolism, catheter damage, necessity review, and home-line safety.

Required safeguards:
- resistant lines are never force-flushed;
- a migrated catheter is never advanced back into the vessel;
- open/disconnected central-line systems with acute symptoms are treated as air-embolism emergencies;
- cracked/leaking catheters are taken out of service and escalated;
- central-line manipulation, declotting, repair, and dressing procedures remain competency/state/facility-policy dependent;
- no source-family lesson is credited as a production row closure without exact learner-reachable lineage.
