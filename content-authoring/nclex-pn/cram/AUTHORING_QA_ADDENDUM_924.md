## 90 — acute skin / soft-tissue infection safety
Ten lessons cover necrotizing soft-tissue infection, cellulitis deterioration, diabetic-foot infection, pressure-injury infection, bite wounds, puncture wounds, water-exposed wounds, abscess/source-control logic, tetanus wound-risk management, and longitudinal wound deterioration.

Safety protections:
- pain out of proportion and rapid progression trigger necrotizing-infection escalation before dramatic necrosis;
- systemic deterioration outranks local skin appearance;
- neuropathy does not rule out severe diabetic-foot infection;
- chronic wounds can seed deep infection and osteomyelitis;
- bite/puncture care includes wound depth, tetanus, infection, and selected rabies considerations;
- antibiotics are not used solely to prevent tetanus;
- source control is recognized when a drainable abscess is present;
- current CDC necrotizing-fasciitis and tetanus-wound guidance anchors the tranche.

## 91 — orthopedic trauma safety
Ten lessons cover open fracture, acute compartment syndrome, pelvic-fracture hemorrhage, fat-embolism deterioration, spinal trauma, geriatric hip fracture, crush injury/rhabdomyolysis, cast-related neurovascular compromise, traumatic amputation, and serial orthopedic neurovascular reassessment.

Safety protections:
- open fractures are treated as contaminated orthopedic emergencies;
- compartment syndrome is escalated before pulse loss;
- pelvic trauma is treated as a potential occult hemorrhage source;
- new hypoxemia/confusion after long-bone trauma triggers embolic deterioration assessment;
- spinal motion restriction and respiratory/perfusion surveillance are preserved;
- hip-fracture care includes delirium, VTE, pressure, and mobility risk;
- crush injury includes hyperkalemia/AKI risk rather than local muscle injury alone;
- worsening pain under a cast triggers neurovascular reassessment rather than reflex analgesia escalation;
- amputated parts are cooled indirectly, never placed directly on ice;
- current AAOS 2025 acute-compartment-syndrome guidance anchors the tranche.
