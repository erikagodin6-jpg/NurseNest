## New tracheostomy / airway-device batch

Ten lessons add tracheostomy obstruction, accidental decannulation, suctioning, humidification/secretions, cuff safety, stoma infection, tracheostomy bleeding, speaking-valve safety, aspiration/swallowing risk, and home emergency readiness.

Safety rules preserved:
- Sudden inability to move air or pass suction is treated as an airway emergency.
- Fresh/immature tracheostomy displacement is never managed by blind forced reinsertion.
- Suctioning is cue-driven, competency governed, and monitored for hypoxemia/trauma.
- Open airway suctioning is treated as a higher-exposure respiratory procedure requiring appropriate infection-control precautions.
- Cuff problems are assessed rather than reflexively corrected with more air.
- Brisk or recurrent tracheostomy bleeding requires emergency evaluation.
- Speaking-valve use requires an open exhalation pathway and prescribed cuff status.
- A tracheostomy does not remove aspiration risk.

## New urinary / urologic-device batch

Ten lessons add indwelling-catheter indication review, closed drainage, obstruction, retention/bladder scanning, intermittent catheterization, suprapubic catheter care, hematuria/clot retention, catheter urine-culture collection, asymptomatic bacteriuria stewardship, and post-removal voiding follow-up.

Safety rules preserved:
- Urinary catheters are used only for appropriate indications and removed when no longer needed.
- Closed drainage, unobstructed flow, securement, and bag-below-bladder positioning are preserved.
- No urine output triggers both device-patency and physiologic evaluation.
- Routine bladder irrigation, antimicrobial instillation, or prophylactic antibiotics are not generalized.
- Catheter urine cultures are collected from the sampling port using aseptic technique rather than from the drainage bag.
- Positive catheter urine culture without compatible symptoms is not automatically labeled symptomatic CAUTI.
- Bladder scanning/intermittent catheterization are framed as alternatives when clinically appropriate and within policy.
