## Clinical review
- Hyperthyroidism: PASS. Thyrotoxicosis is confirmed before etiology; thyroiditis vs hormone overproduction is explicit; storm is a clinical emergency; routine ultrasound is not substituted for functional etiologic testing.
- Thyroid cancer: PASS. Surveillance is histology/risk/response adapted; disease-specific markers and neck imaging are used selectively; long-term TSH suppression is not universal.
- Hyperparathyroidism: PASS. PTH-dependent hypercalcemia is established before localization imaging; FHH/medications/secondary disease are considered.
- Hypoparathyroidism: PASS. Calcium symptom control is balanced against hypercalciuria/renal harm; magnesium and acute ECG/seizure risk are explicit.
- Vitamin D: PASS. Testing is selective and actionable, supplementation avoids megadose toxicity, and CKD/mineral-bone disease is separated from routine deficiency.
- Osteoporosis: PASS. Canadian fracture-risk thresholds and secondary-cause assessment precede therapy; bisphosphonates are first-line for most starting therapy; denosumab discontinuation requires an antiresorptive transition plan.
