## Clinical review
- Polyp surveillance: PASS. Pathology, number, size, completeness and colonoscopy quality determine follow-up; CAG currently lists a new post-polypectomy guideline as on hold/redevelopment, so current regional/endoscopy pathways are required rather than a fabricated universal interval.
- Constipation: PASS. Pattern/medications/secondary causes precede testing; routine colonoscopy for uncomplicated younger low-risk constipation is rejected; pelvic-floor dysfunction is recognized.
- Chronic diarrhea: PASS. Inflammatory, malabsorptive, watery/secretory, medication and functional patterns guide testing; microscopic colitis and bile-acid diarrhea remain visible.
- GI bleeding: PASS. Hemodynamics/perfusion and source control precede the first hemoglobin; variceal versus nonvariceal pathways and individualized antithrombotic reversal/resumption are explicit.
- Dysphagia: PASS. Oropharyngeal versus esophageal localization comes before testing; aspiration and progressive/alarm disease are escalated.
- Anorectal disease: PASS. Hemorrhoids do not automatically explain persistent bleeding/anemia; fissure, abscess/fistula, proctitis, malignancy and IBD are separated.
- Abdominal pain: PASS. Dangerous disposition diagnoses precede diagnostic perfection; pregnancy and extra-abdominal mimics are explicit and analgesia is not withheld to preserve examination findings.
