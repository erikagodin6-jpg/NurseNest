## Clinical review
- **OSA/CPAP:** screening is not diagnosis; home-vs-lab study selection, PAP troubleshooting, sedative risk and driving safety are explicit.
- **Pleural effusion:** syndrome/differential first; diagnostic sampling is not automatic for every bilateral HF effusion; infected collections require definitive source control.
- **Hemoptysis:** airway/physiology outrank memorized volume thresholds; malignancy/TB/PE/anticoagulation are preserved in the differential.
- **Chronic cough:** structured algorithm, ACE inhibitor/exposures/asthma-upper-airway-reflux pathways, and cancer/TB/ILD red flags replace repeated antibiotics/suppressants.
- **Tuberculosis:** TST/IGRA are infection tests, active disease requires imaging/microbiology, preventive therapy never substitutes for exclusion of active disease, and airborne/public-health actions are explicit.
- **Bronchitis vs pneumonia:** routine bronchitis remains non-antibiotic; pneumonia requires objective probability/evidence and severity/disposition assessment.
- **Immunocompromised respiratory infection:** immune defect drives pathogen differential and escalation; muted fever does not reassure.
- **Rhinitis/sinusitis:** allergic inflammation and viral rhinosinusitis are separated from bacterial patterns; antibiotic stewardship and orbital/intracranial red flags are explicit.
