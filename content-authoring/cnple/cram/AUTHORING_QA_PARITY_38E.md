## Clinical audit

### Metabolic alkalosis / mixed disorders

PASS. Chloride/volume and potassium physiology are explicit; mineralocorticoid causes remain in the differential. Mixed disorders are detected by inappropriate compensation and clinical context; normal pH is not treated as normal acid-base physiology.

### Sepsis pattern

PASS. No biomarker is permitted to diagnose sepsis alone. Infection plus organ dysfunction and bedside trajectory drive urgency. Lactate, WBC, creatinine, platelets and coagulation are supporting severity signals only.

### DKA pattern

PASS. Ketosis/acidosis—not glucose alone—defines treatment progress. Potassium-aware insulin, euglycemic DKA, precipitant identification and ketone/acidosis resolution are preserved.

### GI bleeding

PASS. Hemodynamic status precedes hemoglobin. Early normal hemoglobin does not rule out acute hemorrhage; FOBT/FIT is not used diagnostically for overt/suspected bleeding. Transfusion is clinical/status based and restrictive in stable nonmassive bleeding rather than driven by one universal number.

### Heart failure

PASS. Natriuretic peptide, renal function, sodium and potassium are interpreted beside congestion/perfusion and treatment. A small creatinine rise during effective decongestion is not automatically treated as treatment failure.

### Shock

PASS. Shock is inadequate tissue perfusion rather than a lactate diagnosis. The lesson distinguishes hemorrhagic, distributive, cardiogenic and obstructive causes and requires post-intervention reassessment.

### Multi-lab judgment

PASS. Priority is determined by physiologic threat, change from baseline and treatment response rather than the numerically most abnormal result. Closed-loop ownership of critical/pending results is explicit.
