## Clinical audit

### CBC / coagulation

PASS. CBC interpretation is pattern-based across cell lines, indices, differential and trend. Platelet count/function and coagulation-factor testing are kept distinct. PT/aPTT are not presented as universal bleeding screens or reliable quantitative DOAC assays.

### Calcium / magnesium

PASS. The lessons distinguish total versus biologically active calcium context, include magnesium as a cause of refractory hypocalcemia/hypokalemia, and prioritize symptoms/ECG/renal function over one number. Severe hypermagnesemia is tied to renal impairment/exposure and cardiopulmonary depression.

### CK / CK-MB

PASS. Total CK is retained for skeletal-muscle injury/rhabdomyolysis. CK-MB is explicitly not taught as a routine companion to contemporary troponin in ACS evaluation. Hyperkalemia/AKI/compartment-syndrome risk remains central in severe muscle breakdown.

### Acid-base

PASS. Respiratory acidosis is taught as effective-ventilation failure, not an oxygenation problem. Respiratory alkalosis keeps PE, sepsis, pregnancy and toxicology in the differential rather than defaulting to anxiety. Metabolic acidosis uses anion gap plus compensation to find the cause and mixed disorders; bicarbonate therapy is not presented as universal treatment.

## Current Canadian source anchors rechecked

- Choosing Wisely Canada Cardiology recommendations / CK-MB stewardship: prefer troponin I/T rather than CK-MB/myoglobin for myocardial injury evaluation.
- Choosing Wisely Canada Using Labs Wisely / Medical Laboratory Science recommendations: focused coagulation/laboratory testing and avoidance of low-value repeat testing.
- Emergency Care BC hypocalcemia/electrolyte resources: symptom/ECG-driven treatment and correction of associated magnesium abnormalities.
- Diabetes Canada hyperglycemic-emergency guidance for DKA acid-base/potassium context.
