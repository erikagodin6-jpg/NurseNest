## Clinical review

### Pulmonary function testing
PASS. Spirometry, lung volumes and diffusion capacity are separated by physiologic role. Low FVC alone is not labelled restriction; bronchodilator response supports but does not independently diagnose/exclude asthma; test quality and current reference standards are required.

### Oxygen therapy
PASS. Oxygen is prescribed for hypoxemia with a target/reassessment plan, not for dyspnea alone. Hypercapnia risk, fire safety and the difference between acute oxygen and long-term home eligibility are explicit.

### Lung cancer screening
PASS. Screening is restricted to asymptomatic high-risk people using low-dose CT through current Canadian/provincial pathways. Symptoms trigger diagnostic evaluation. Chest radiography/sputum are not substituted for LDCT screening, and provincial program differences are flagged.

### Allergic rhinitis / sinusitis
PASS. Intranasal anti-inflammatory therapy and trigger management are separated from acute rhinosinusitis. Purulent mucus alone is not a bacterial diagnosis; uncomplicated viral disease does not receive reflex antibiotics or routine imaging.

### Acute bronchitis vs pneumonia
PASS. Vitals, oxygenation, focal findings and host risk drive imaging/antibiotic decisions. Sputum colour does not determine bacterial disease. Older/frail/immunocompromised atypical presentations are preserved.

### Occupational lung disease
PASS. Exact task/exposure/latency/work-versus-away timing is part of the diagnostic history. Exposure control is not replaced by symptom medication, and legal/compensation causation is not asserted from one clinical encounter.

### Obstructive sleep apnea
PASS. Screening tools estimate probability; sleep testing establishes diagnosis. PAP troubleshooting, sedative/opioid risk, driving safety and alternate causes of sleepiness are explicit.

### Pulmonary nodule
PASS. Size/morphology/growth and patient risk drive follow-up. Screening-program, incidental and symptomatic diagnostic pathways are separated, and follow-up ownership is explicit.
