## Clinical audit

### Bronchiectasis
PASS. CT confirms structural disease; airway clearance, exacerbation frequency, sputum microbiology and underlying-cause evaluation are explicit. Chronic antibiotics/inhaled strategies remain specialist/culture guided.

### Pulmonary hypertension
PASS. Pulmonary hypertension is treated as a hemodynamic syndrome with distinct etiologic groups. Echo supports probability but does not justify empiric PAH-specific vasodilator treatment; CTEPH and right-heart failure are explicit referral priorities.

### Sarcoidosis
PASS. Compatible clinical/radiographic pattern plus exclusion of infectious/malignant mimics is required. Treatment is organ-risk/function driven rather than automatic corticosteroids for granulomas or hilar adenopathy.

### Adult cystic fibrosis
PASS. CF is framed as multidisciplinary lifelong disease through a Canadian CF centre, including pulmonary exacerbations, microbiology, airway clearance, CFTR-modulator interactions, CFRD, liver/bone/nutrition and reproductive/mental-health care. Acute non-CF clinicians are instructed to preserve CF-centre continuity.

### Respiratory infection in immunocompromise
PASS. Differential and testing are tied to the specific immune defect. Muted fever/normal initial radiography cannot falsely reassure a high-risk host; opportunistic infection and noninfectious mimics remain in the differential.

### Oxygen therapy
PASS. Oxygen is prescribed for hypoxemia with an explicit target and reassessment. Oxygenation and ventilation are separate; chronic hypercapnic risk does not justify withholding oxygen from severe hypoxemia, while home oxygen requires documented program criteria.

### Mechanical ventilation
PASS. Oxygenation, ventilation, mechanics, equipment and underlying disease are separated. Sudden deterioration triggers patient/tube/circuit/pneumothorax assessment before blind setting escalation. Lung-protective principles and weaning readiness are preserved.

### Asthma-COPD overlap
PASS. Spirometry/phenotype are required rather than using wheeze/age alone. When asthma is truly present, ICS-containing therapy is retained and LABA monotherapy is avoided; COPD symptom/exacerbation burden still drives bronchodilator and rehabilitation strategy.
