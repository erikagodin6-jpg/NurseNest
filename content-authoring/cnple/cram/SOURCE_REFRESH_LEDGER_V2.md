# CNPLE Cram Library — Canadian Source Refresh Ledger V2

Refresh date: 2026-08-07
Scope: Canadian Nurse Practitioner / CNPLE entry-level cram library

This ledger identifies the source families that govern high-change clinical content. It is a publication safety control: a lesson may remain clinically sound for years, but the source family below must be rechecked whenever a new national guideline, drug safety change, immunization statement, public-health regimen, screening program, or professional standard is released.

## Exam and NP competency scope

- College of Nurses of Ontario (CNO): Nurse Practitioner registration and single NP classification implementation effective 2026-07-01.
- Canadian Council of Registered Nurse Regulators (CCRNR): Nurse Practitioner Entry-Level Competencies and CNPLE blueprint/competency framework.
- Refresh trigger: any new CNPLE blueprint, NP entry-to-practice competency set, registration exam transition, or national NP regulation change.

## Cardiovascular

- Hypertension Canada: 2025 Primary Care Guideline.
- Canadian Cardiovascular Society / Canadian Heart Rhythm Society: atrial fibrillation guidance and focused updates.
- Canadian Cardiovascular Society: heart failure guidance and focused updates.
- Canadian Stroke Best Practices: acute stroke/TIA, secondary prevention and rehabilitation; current 2025-2026 modules.
- Thrombosis Canada: VTE diagnosis/treatment/duration, cancer-associated thrombosis, DOAC/warfarin bleeding and peri-procedural guides; current 2025-2026 guides.
- Refresh trigger: threshold, target, antithrombotic, reperfusion-window, anticoagulant-dose, VTE-duration or foundational-HF-treatment change.

## Diabetes, endocrine and metabolic

- Diabetes Canada: 2024 pharmacologic management of type 2 diabetes; 2025 type 1 diabetes, chronic kidney disease and MASLD guidance where applicable.
- Osteoporosis Canada: current fracture-risk and treatment guidance.
- Canadian endocrine specialty guidance for thyroid, adrenal, pituitary and metabolic emergencies.
- Refresh trigger: diagnostic threshold, cardiorenal indication, pregnancy recommendation, glucose technology recommendation, osteoporosis treatment sequence or drug safety change.

## Respiratory and sleep

- Canadian Thoracic Society: asthma, COPD, alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency, sleep and interstitial-lung-disease guidance; use the most recent CTS statement available for the specific topic.
- Refresh trigger: inhaler strategy, SABA/ICS recommendation, COPD pharmacotherapy, oxygen, pulmonary rehabilitation, biologic indication or sleep-testing pathway change.

## Pediatrics and newborn care

- Canadian Paediatric Society (CPS), including current resources for:
  - well-appearing febrile infants 90 days or younger — updated 2026;
  - acute croup — updated 2026;
  - acute otitis media — current/reaffirmed guidance;
  - newborn hyperbilirubinemia — current 2025 guidance;
  - newborn hypoglycemia — updated 2025;
  - bronchiolitis;
  - pediatric acute kidney injury — 2025;
  - acute head trauma — 2025;
  - Kawasaki disease — current 2025-2026 resource;
  - autism early detection and post-diagnostic management.
- Refresh trigger: age cut-off, inflammatory-marker algorithm, lumbar-puncture threshold, bilirubin phototherapy threshold, hypoglycemia threshold, antibiotic recommendation or developmental screening change.

## Obstetric, gynecology and reproductive health

- Society of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists of Canada (SOGC): current pregnancy, preconception, hypertensive disorders, postpartum, endometriosis, recurrent pregnancy loss, menopause and gynecologic guidance.
- SOGC ALARM program: current emergency obstetric principles; 2026 materials where applicable.
- Provincial or territorial prenatal and cervical-screening programs for operational eligibility and pathways.
- Refresh trigger: screening program, aspirin indication, pregnancy medication, Rh prophylaxis, recurrent-loss investigation, cervical HPV program or emergency obstetric recommendation change.

## Mental health

- CANMAT:
  - Major Depressive Disorder guideline 2023;
  - Bipolar disorder current guidance;
  - Perinatal Mood, Anxiety and Related Disorders guideline 2024;
  - OCD guideline 2025.
- Canadian early-psychosis, suicide-prevention, eating-disorder and trauma-informed practice resources where no single national CANMAT guideline governs the topic.
- Refresh trigger: first-line medication/psychotherapy sequence, pregnancy recommendation, suicide-risk practice or major drug-safety change.

## Substance use and addiction medicine

- CRISM National Guideline for Clinical Management of Opioid Use Disorder — 2024 update: buprenorphine and methadone standard first-line OAT; slow-release oral morphine second-line when appropriate.
- Canadian guideline for high-risk drinking and alcohol use disorder — 2023.
- Canada's Guidance on Alcohol and Health — 2023 and later national updates.
- Health Canada and Canadian harm-reduction resources.
- Refresh trigger: OAT initiation strategy, safer-supply policy affecting clinical practice, AUD pharmacotherapy, withdrawal pathway or national lower-risk guidance change.

## Infectious disease, STBBI and public health

- Public Health Agency of Canada (PHAC) STBBI Guides — current June 2026 updates where applicable.
- Gonorrhea: use the June 2026 PHAC preferred regimen and current culture/test-of-cure guidance rather than older memorized doses.
- PHAC syphilis, chlamydia, genital herpes, HIV biomedical prevention, hepatitis, Lyme, travel-health and outbreak resources.
- Canadian antimicrobial-stewardship and specialty infectious-disease resources.
- Refresh trigger: antimicrobial regimen, resistance pattern, test-of-cure, partner-management, PrEP/PEP, reportable-disease or outbreak recommendation change.

## Immunization

- National Advisory Committee on Immunization (NACI) and Canadian Immunization Guide — use current 2026 national guidance.
- Provincial or territorial schedules determine publicly funded products, intervals and operational access when they differ from national clinical recommendations.
- Refresh trigger: product approval, dose interval, age/risk indication, RSV/COVID/influenza/pneumococcal/zoster recommendation, pregnancy recommendation or immunocompromised-host timing change.

## Oncology and hematology

- Cancer Care Ontario and Canadian oncology survivorship/emergency resources.
- Thrombosis Canada for cancer-associated thrombosis.
- Canadian hematology and Myeloma Canada resources where relevant.
- Refresh trigger: cancer emergency management, surveillance, thrombosis, transfusion or therapy-related late-effect recommendation change.

## Toxicology and environmental medicine

- Canadian Association for Poison Centres and Clinical Toxicology and regional Canadian poison centres.
- Ontario Poison Centre revised IV N-acetylcysteine protocol effective 2026-04-01, aligned with Canadian poison-centre standards. Do not restore obsolete universal NAC infusion schedules during downstream editing.
- Health Canada extreme-heat and environmental-health resources.
- Refresh trigger: antidote protocol, toxic concentration threshold, observation period, antidote stocking or poison-centre consensus change.

## MAID, consent and professional law

- Criminal Code of Canada MAID framework.
- Health Canada Model Practice Standard for MAID and current federal reporting/eligibility information.
- As of 2026-08, mental illness as the sole underlying medical condition remains excluded from MAID eligibility until 2027-03-17 unless Parliament changes the law before then.
- CNO and the applicable provincial/territorial nursing regulator for NP scope, prescribing, controlled substances, virtual care, consent/capacity, documentation and professional obligations.
- Local law/policy must be checked for mandatory reporting, public-health notification, substitute decision-making, driving, adolescent consent, adult protection, child protection and controlled-substance requirements.
- Refresh trigger: any legislative, regulatory or court-driven change.

## Screening and publicly funded prevention programs

- National evidence sources provide clinical guidance, but breast, cervical, colorectal, lung and some other screening eligibility/program details vary by province or territory and change over time.
- Lessons with program-specific screening content must keep `localPolicyCheck: true` unless the statement is truly national and stable.
- Symptomatic patients must never be routed through a routine screening interval when diagnostic evaluation is indicated.

## Publication rule

Before a production release, recheck every lesson whose `sourceBasis` belongs to a source family above if:

1. the source has released a newer guideline or focused update;
2. the lesson contains an exact drug regimen, dose, interval, threshold or legal rule;
3. the lesson has `localPolicyCheck: true` and the implementation depends on province/territory, formulary, reporting law or funded program;
4. the lesson has not been source-refreshed within the period appropriate to its rate of change; or
5. a downstream editor changed the clinical wording after this audit.

A current source is part of the content contract, not optional bibliography.
