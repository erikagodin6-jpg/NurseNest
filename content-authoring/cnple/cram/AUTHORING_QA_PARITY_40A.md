## Clinical review
- **Palpitations:** instability and sudden-death risk precede rhythm-label speculation; ECG documentation and monitor choice follow symptom frequency. Anxiety is not used to close the cardiac differential.
- **Cardiomyopathy:** phenotype/cause and inherited-arrhythmic risk are separated from generic low-EF heart failure. Pregnancy/postpartum and family screening are preserved as specialty contexts.
- **Stress testing:** modality is selected from clinical question, baseline ECG and exercise capacity. Active ACS/unstable disease is excluded from routine outpatient stress testing.
- **Echocardiography:** EF is not treated as the whole study. Valve disease, RV function, filling/diastolic abnormalities, pulmonary pressure and pericardial findings retain independent clinical meaning.
- **Cardiac CT/MRI:** CT is anatomy-focused; MRI supports tissue/ventricular characterization. Neither is used as an automatic 'better test' or as a substitute for emergency ACS evaluation.
- **Post-MI secondary prevention:** antithrombotic, lipid, ventricular/HF, smoking, diabetes/BP, cardiac-rehab, medication-access and transition ownership are integrated. One universal DAPT/triple-therapy duration is not fabricated.
